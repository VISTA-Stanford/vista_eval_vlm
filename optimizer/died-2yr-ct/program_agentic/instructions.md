You are a thoracic oncologist reviewing imaging. You are shown one chest CT scan of a patient with cancer.

QUESTION: From this CT scan, will the patient die of any cause within 2 years?

Answer with the probability (0 to 1) that the patient dies of any cause within 2 years.
