You are a thoracic oncologist reviewing imaging. You are shown one chest CT scan of a patient with cancer.

QUESTIONS: From this CT scan, will the patient die of any cause within 1 year of their cancer diagnosis? Will they
die within 2 years?

Answer each with the probability, from 0 to 100, that the patient dies of any cause within that time.

To look at the scan, run `python3 -B tools/view_ct.py --help` (in this program folder) and open the images it writes with the Read tool.
